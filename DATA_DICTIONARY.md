# Data dictionary

## Notation and units

`GB` means GazeBase (desktop, nominal 1000 Hz); `GBVR` means GazeBaseVR (headset, nominal 250 Hz). The study analyses round 1 random-saccade (`RAN`) recordings. `D` is development and `C` is confirmatory; they are disjoint **within each dataset**, not paired participants across datasets. The fixed sizes are GB D=100/C=222 and GBVR D=50/C=357.

`pid` and recording/member names are public upstream pseudonyms, not names or newly assigned identities. `mid` is a **measurement-chain member index**, not a participant. `name`/`A_names` commonly identify a chain such as `B1_bw4_30_post_a1`. Positions are in degrees of visual angle; peak velocity (`pv`, `pv10`, `PV@10°`) is in degrees/second. Time values labelled `ms` are milliseconds; `wall_s` and `cpu_s` are execution seconds. Logs and sample-level trajectories are not included.

## Common JSON fields

`A` is the retained/admissible member set. `Lambda`, `c` and `Lambda_over_c` are the calibration test statistic, critical value and their ratio. `D_hat`, `z`, `eta` and `native_coords` describe calibrated coordinate differences, standardised coordinates and noise parameters, not raw gaze samples. The 12 coordinates are ordered fixation scale/spread (1–4), clean-window spectral/autocorrelation summaries (5–10), and missingness (11–12); the `names`/`z_names` fields give the exact order.

`decision`, `tag`, `retained`, `oracle`, `coverage_*`, `false_eq` and `false_mm` describe the benchmark decision/oracle/coverage results. `truth`, `rep`, `quarters`, `seed`, `streams` and `config` preserve the design and resampling structure. `lo`/`hi` and `ci95_deg_s` are intervals, whose level and estimand must be read with the enclosing result and paper/table labels. Native PV intervals are 95%; native-ratio bootstrap bands are 90%.

`_provisional`, `provisional`, `note`, `notes`, `_provenance` and `deviations` are scientific provenance, retained unchanged. A provisional marker does not always mean an unfinished estimate: read `README_DEPOSIT.md`. `_resources`, `timings`, `wall_s` and `cpu_h` are execution summaries without host addresses or private paths. JSON may contain `NaN` for unavailable statistics; Python's standard JSON reader accepts these historical values. Do not silently convert missing/non-estimable results to zero.

## NPZ files

Load with `numpy.load(path, allow_pickle=False)`.

| File | Arrays |
| --- | --- |
| `A006/c_adm_Db.npz` | `mid`: int64 `(15,)`; `Db`: float32 `(15, 2000, 12)`; `names`: Unicode `(12,)` |
| `A006/d_adm_Db.npz` | `mid`: int64 `(276,)`; `Db`: float32 `(276, 2000, 12)`; `names`: Unicode `(12,)` |

The axes of `Db` are chain member × participant-bootstrap replicate × hold coordinate. These are aggregated bootstrap coordinate differences, **not a participant or time-sample axis**. `names` holds coordinate labels. No object/pickle array is distributed.

## Exhaustive machine-readable schema

`provenance/data_inventory.json` records every result/plot-input JSON's root keys and normalized nested field paths (including scalar types and array structure), and every NPZ array's dtype, shape and role. Numeric dictionary keys representing IDs/member indices are normalized to `{index}` in the schema only; the actual scientific files and their identifiers are unchanged. The complete inventory and data privacy check were generated by `tools/audit_public_data.py`.
