The present derived-data release changes the representation through the documented analysis, aggregation and bootstrap procedures. It does not redistribute demographics or raw gaze rows. Official public pseudonyms and recording-member references are retained for frozen scientific provenance. No new identifying key is included, and no attempt to re-identify a person is part of this work. A CC licence does not by itself settle privacy or other third-party rights.
